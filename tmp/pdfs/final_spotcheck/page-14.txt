A CONTROLLABILITYEXPERIMENTDETAILS
A.1 QUESTIONS AND EXPERIMENTAL COVERAGE
We draw multiple-choice questions from CommonsenseQA (CSQA), ARC-Challenge (ARC-C),
GPQA, MMLU-Pro, and Humanity’s Last Exam (HLE). Table 1 reports the question mixtures ac-
tually used in the reported analyses. Counts refer to distinct question–control pairs: a question
evaluated under two controls contributes twice, while repeated samples, conditions, and instruction
phrasings do not increase the count.
The eight-control suites share 240 question–control pairs covering 178 distinct questions. Sentence
numbering and sentence endings reuse the 30 sentence-prefix questions; sentence endings also reuse
their marker assignments. The opening concentration/entropy analysis contains 119 distinct ques-
tions, and the four-control rank analysis contains 108. Question–control pairs are shared across
models within each analysis.
Elicitation questions are selected from a pool with existing reasoning traces and suitable com-
pliant examples. Sampling is not stratified by dataset, and dataset proportions vary across con-
trol tasks. The rank analysis instead reuses saved responses to prompts sampled from a separate
GPQA/HLE/MMLU-Pro pool. Thus, question mixtures are matched within experiments but are not
uniform across the paper.
A.2 CONTROL TASKS AND SCORING
Our main elicitation and correlation analyses use eight controls across information addition, in-
formation suppression, and stylistic modification. Table 2 summarizes their response-level scores.
Compliance is evaluated independently of final-answer correctness.
Sentence boundaries are punctuation marks (.!?) followed by whitespace. Prefix matching is case-
sensitive. Language grading uses Gemini-2.5-Flash-Lite, instructed to assess linguistic compliance
rather than correctness and to treat formulas, code, and proper nouns as language-neutral. First-
person grading exempts uppercase “US” and dotted abbreviations and does not prohibit “let’s.”
Markers (for Sentence prefixes and Sentence endings):BEEP,BOOP,CRUNCH,DING,GLOOP,
NOTE,PING,SNAP,WHIZ,YAP,ZORP.
