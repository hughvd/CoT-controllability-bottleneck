ETHICS STATEMENT
Our work aims to improve the assessment of chain-of-thought monitoring by identifying limitations
in current controllability benchmarks. Methods that increase control over reasoning traces could
also be used to make those traces less informative or misleading, although our experiments do not
establish deliberate monitor evasion. We believe that openly studying these limitations can support
more reliable evaluations and reduce unwarranted confidence in the safety assurances drawn from
low measured controllability.
REPRODUCIBILITY STATEMENT
We describe the models, datasets, prompts, grading rules, and experimental settings in the main text
and appendix. Upon publication, we will provide a link to a public repository containing the code
and experiment configurations, including scripts for scoring responses and reproducing the reported
analyses and figures.
