Think, and GLM-5.2 and evaluate controllability using uppercase, leetspeak, Spanish, and word
omission tasks. For each model and prefix length, we identify the most common opening prefix
without instructions and measure the fraction of responses matching that same prefix under both
conditions. We also compute next-token entropy for Qwen and OLMo along their sampled reason-
