How to read this

Controllability is instruction compliance at K=0 in the existing 12-model sweep. Soft means average the saved compliance scores; hard means the fraction scoring ≥0.95. Correlations use paired models, not individual generated responses. These are descriptive associations with small, related model samples and many comparisons—not causal effects or significance claims.

Intervention uses g-mean; process uses TPR; outcome uses g-mean. “CoT only” and “All messages” refer to monitor input. The composite equally weights intervention mean, CoT process TPR, and CoT outcome g-mean. Restricted score ranges and floor/ceiling counts are shown on the plots; use the observed-range zoom with care.

Category mapping, coverage, and source provenance

Addition: sentence prefixes, numbered sentences, and sentence endings. Suppression: omit word and no first person. Style: uppercase, leetspeak, and Spanish. Chinese and persona styles are excluded. Numbering and sentence endings use new runs on the sentence-prefix target questions; the other six tasks reuse historical results.

Category means weight control types equally. Type means weight valid responses equally, matching the existing sweep loader. Missing/error/invalid scores are excluded; coverage below shows the valid sample counts. Historical model, reasoning-effort, and backend choices are inherited from each source run.
