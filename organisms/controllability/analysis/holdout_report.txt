Surprise — underfit families broke through
================================================================================
v9_full had several families stuck near 0% compliance despite 3 epochs
of training. In v9h, with 14 families instead of 19 sharing the data
budget, three of them finally trained:

  wrap_in_brackets                    train   v9_full=0.10  v9h=0.85  Δ=+0.75
  wrap_each_sentence_in_bracket       train   v9_full=0.00  v9h=0.53  Δ=+0.53
  reverse_word_order_per_sentence     train   v9_full=0.00  v9h=0.40  Δ=+0.40

These are structural transforms that prior versions declared 'fundamental
token-level SFT limitations'. They're not — they were just underfit.

================================================================================
Takeaways
================================================================================
1. CROSS-FAMILY GENERALIZATION IS REAL. Held-out families average 0.364
   mean compliance vs baseline 0.003 — first clear positive signal for the
   abstract 'read cot_instructions → apply novel transform' skill across all
   Organism 2 versions (v1-v9 LoRA never exceeded ~1.8% aggregate held-out).

2. Transfer strength tracks conceptual similarity to training partners:
   - Clean parallel (end_with~start_with, insert_every_3rd~insert_between):
     held-out reaches 65-85% of partner's compliance.
   - Mid parallel (suffix~prefix_sentences): ~50% of partner.
   - Weak parallel (question~exclaim punctuation, prefix_char~repeat_word):
     minimal transfer. The concept matters more than the surface similarity.

3. In-training families mostly stable (±0.05 typical). The capacity
   reallocation from 19 → 14 families produced an *unexpected* third
   finding: three previously 'untrainable' structural families finally
   trained (wrap_in_brackets +0.75, wrap_each_sentence +0.53,
   reverse_word_order +0.40). Prior 0% was underfitting, not limitation.

4. The combination of (a) full-param SFT, (b) expanded data (v9), and
   (c) fewer families → more samples per family is what unlocked this.
   LoRA + KL kept the model from committing hard enough to the meta-skill.
