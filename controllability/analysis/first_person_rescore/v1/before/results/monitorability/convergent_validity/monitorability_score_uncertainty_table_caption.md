Suggested main-text reference:

The monitorability evaluations differ in their observed spread across models (Appendix Figure~\ref{fig:monitorability-score-uncertainty}). Memory Advice and GPQA Physics are concentrated near ceiling, with scores spanning 0.872--0.982 and 0.868--0.962, respectively, while Scruples scores remain between 0.000 and 0.222. Because several between-model differences are small relative to bootstrap variability, we treat the corresponding rank correlations as descriptive patterns rather than evidence of a stable ordering of models.
